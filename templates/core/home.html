Why PSDR

Everything a modern parking system should be

Built for Rwanda's busiest venues. Live detection, honest pricing, MoMo-ready payments, and a driver dashboard that actually helps.

Real-time slot detection

Sensors and scan logs keep each slot's status live. No more guessing if there is space.

MoMo & Airtel payments

Pay for your slot in seconds. Extend your stay with a single tap when you are on the road.

15-minute expiry alert

We warn you exactly 15 minutes before your booking ends, with an extend option.

Categories that matter

Economic, Business, Premium. Pay only for the safety and services you actually use.

Plate scan entry & exit

Camera scans your plate on the way in and out. Slots free themselves automatically.

Built for events too

Event hosters can bulk-book entire parkings and offer shuttle transport from pickup points.
